SyncOn Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

What SyncOn processes

SyncOn processes Android app identifiers and Chrome domain names, focused-use intervals, categories, limits, block events, installation identifiers, and—when optional sync is enabled—an account email and connected-device metadata. It does not collect page contents, full URLs, searches, forms, keystrokes, contacts, precise location, payment data, or advertising identifiers.

Local-first and connected sync

Android tracking and blocking work without the Chrome extension or an internet connection. Chrome activates only after one successful QR pairing from Android; after activation, temporary network outages do not stop its local tracking or blocking. Android uses Room and Chrome uses extension-owned IndexedDB. Paired activity and settings synchronize through encrypted Supabase connections protected by per-account Row Level Security.

Use and sharing

Data is used only for screen-time reports, user-configured limits, pairing, synchronization, security, and reliability. SyncOn does not sell data, show ads, or perform cross-site tracking. Supabase supplies authentication/database infrastructure and Google Play Services supplies Android QR scanning.

Retention and control

Local history is retained for up to three years unless cleared sooner. Users can export local data, disconnect devices, clear Chrome history, sign out, and permanently delete the cloud account from Android. Cloud deletion does not silently erase local history stored on separate installations.

Security

Pairing QR codes expire and are single-use. They contain no password or access token. The backend stores only a hash of the pairing secret. Sync uses encrypted transport, account-scoped authorization, rate limits, audit events, conflict detection, and private Realtime topics.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the SyncOn issue tracker. Never post passwords, QR secrets, tokens, or private usage exports.
